How to Play

Objective: Guide the red ball to the green goal for as many rounds as you can before time runs out!

Controls:

  • Press A to rotate the maze counterclockwise
  • Press D to rotate the maze clockwise

Gameplay:

  • Complete each round to get +15 seconds and +1000 points
  • Your score decreases over time, so be quick!
  • The game continues endlessly until time runs out
